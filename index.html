About the doctor

A dermatologist who explains what she's looking at.

Elena trained in general medicine before specialising in dermatology, and still treats the consultation as the main event: what the skin looks like today, what it looked like six months ago, and what changed in between. Diagnostic work runs alongside minor surgery — biopsies, excisions, cryotherapy — in the same room, usually in the same visit.

If a biopsy is needed, results are discussed by phone within two weeks rather than left to a letter.
